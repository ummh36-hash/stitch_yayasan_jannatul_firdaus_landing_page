## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an elevated fusion of classical Islamic cultural heritage and contemporary digital minimalism. Designed for an Islamic foundation and dakwah center, the visual language balances solemnity, intellectual depth, and generous hospitality. It invites donors, seekers of knowledge, and community members into a tranquil, sacredly grounded modern digital space.

The aesthetic philosophy centers on:
- **Serene Elevation:** Abundant negative space reminiscent of traditional courtyard architecture (sahn), allowing content to breathe and resonate.
- **Dignified Refinement:** Expressive, classic editorial headlines paired with exceptionally legible contemporary body typography.
- **Sacred Accents:** Deliberate, understated applications of warm amber-gold and deep botanical emerald, eschewing gaudy ornamentation in favor of purposeful, luminous accents.
- **Architectural Symmetry & Harmony:** Subdued geometric underpinnings, soft borders, and gentle tonal elevations that honor historic Islamic craftsmanship without compromising digital utility.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from heritage manuscripts, courtyard gardens, and artisanal brasswork, framed within clean, light-filled modern surfaces.

### Primary & Accent Roles
- **Primary Emerald (`#064E3B`):** Represents heritage, balance, and quiet authority. Used for prominent brand headers, primary call-to-action buttons, key banners, and active navigation states.
- **Secondary Gold (`#D97706`):** Evokes illuminated manuscripts and brass lanterns. Used selectively for highlighting dakwah initiatives, pledge goals, honorary badges, and high-priority interactive cues.
- **Tertiary Meadow (`#047857`):** A vibrant garden green used for interactive hover states, secondary highlights, success badges, and prayer time indicators.

### Surfaces & Backgrounds
- **Primary Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast cards, focal modals, and content containers.
- **Pristine Slate Foundation (`#F8FAFC`):** The primary canvas background, creating clean separation without sterile glare.
- **Tranquil Tint (`#F0FDF4`):** An ethereal mint-ivory background used for featured announcement blocks, community callouts, and quote cards.
- **Warm Gold Tint (`#FEF3C7`):** Background wash for gold accent tags, zakaat alerts, and donor milestone cards.

### Text & Neutral Architecture
- **Deep Charcoal Text (`#0F172A`):** Delivers optimal contrast and warmth for long-form reading, replacing harsh pure black.
- **Muted Slate (`#475569`):** Subtitles, meta-information, timestamps, and secondary captions.
- **Subtle Border Line (`#E2E8F0`):** Soft, structural division for cards, dividers, and input borders.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between formal reverence and accessible modern clarity. 

- **Playfair Display (Headlines & Editorial Callouts):** Bestows stature and historical grace upon major milestones, program titles, hadith or Quranic translations, and narrative sections. Its high contrast and sculpted serifs reflect classic bookbinding and lithography.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body, Interface, & Navigation):** Delivers clean geometry, crisp horizontal metrics, and high legibility across mobile touchscreens and desktop views alike.
- **Arabic Script Pairing:** When bilingual Arabic text is embedded, Arabic characters must render at roughly 110–120% scale relative to the Latin font size to preserve visual balance and baseline harmony.
- **Editorial Quotations:** Blockquotes featuring spiritual maxims or scripture translations employ `headline-sm` in italicized Playfair Display, paired with an emerald left border and gold opening quotation accents.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout draws inspiration from traditional architectural symmetry—wide, airy, disciplined, and calm.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column responsive fluid grid with max-width constrained to `1280px` for optimal reading proportion. Gutters are fixed to `1.5rem` (`24px`) with page margins expanding up to `3rem` on wide displays.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` screen margins.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` screen margins, ensuring content edges never bleed to the glass edge.

### Layout Rhythms
- **Canvas Cadence:** Maintain intentional breathing room between distinct dakwah initiatives, daily schedules, and prayer boards using `space-xl` as vertical module separators.
- **Alignment:** Content leans toward centered symmetry for hero narratives, opening hadith statements, and pledge goals, transitioning into disciplined left-aligned (or right-aligned for RTL locales) multi-column grids for event cards, lecture archives, and financial transparency ledgers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects plastic, artificial drop shadows in favor of ambient, emerald-tinted atmospheric diffusion combined with soft architectural borders.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Applied to the foundation background (`#F8FAFC`). No depth or shadow.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Cards & Lists):** Surface background `#FFFFFF` overlaid with a hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and an extra-soft ambient shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Cards & Popovers):** Elevated cards on hover or active filter states: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.07), 0 3px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Borders transition subtly to an emerald tint (`#A7F3D0`).
- **Level 3 (Modals & Donation Trays):** High-priority overlays use: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`. Backdrops use a frosted veil (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.45)` with `blur(8px)`).
- **Subtle Islamic Motifs:** Depth may be enriched through translucent, vector-drawn geometric arabesques or 8-point star starbursts (watermarked at `3%` to `5%` opacity) layered behind major banners and card headers.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced, refined curvature (`roundedness: 2`) that conveys warmth and approachable community engagement while retaining crisp structure.

- **Standard Elements (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to input fields, buttons, regular chips, and nested list items.
- **Containers & Cards (1rem / 16px):** Applied to program cards, prayer time displays, donation calculators, and content modules.
- **Featured Panels & Modals (1.5rem / 24px):** Used for focal banner highlights, community announcement containers, and central modal dialogues.
- **Pill Badges (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for status indicators, categorization chips (e.g., "Quran Study", "Youth Circle"), and donation milestone tags.
- **Architectural Archway Motifs:** Hero image frames and featured scholar profiles may feature an arched top edge (`border-radius: 9999px 9999px 16px 16px` or a pointed ogee contour) as a nod to traditional Islamic architecture.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Background in deep emerald green (`#064E3B`), text in pure white (`#FFFFFF`), font weight `600`. Hover state transitions to `#047857` with subtle Level 2 elevation.
- **Secondary Button:** Surface in white (`#FFFFFF`), border `1.5px solid #064E3B`, text in `#064E3B`. Hover state fills with `#F0FDF4`.
- **Gold Accent (Giving/Donation CTA):** Background in warm gold (`#D97706`), text in white (`#FFFFFF`), font weight `700`. Hover transitions to `#B45309`.
- **Text Link Button:** Deep emerald with subtle underline on hover, paired with a rightward directional arrow.

### Cards
- **Refined Content Card:** `#FFFFFF` background, `16px` border-radius, soft outline (`1px solid #E2E8F0`), and Level 1 elevation. Padding is generous (`24px`).
- **Featured Dakwah Card:** Subtle gradient or background in ivory-mint (`#F0FDF4`), with an optional gold top-border accent strip (`3px solid #D97706`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Category Badges:** Pill-shaped, `12px` font size with uppercase letter-spacing. 
  - *Heritage Gold Chip:* `#FEF3C7` background with `#92400E` text.
  - *Emerald Activity Chip:* `#DCFCE7` background with `#065F46` text.
- **Filter Chips:** Inactive in `#F8FAFC` background with `#475569` text and `#E2E8F0` border; active states fill with `#064E3B` and white text.

### Form Inputs
- **Text Inputs & Dropdowns:** `8px` rounded corners, `1.5px solid #E2E8F0` border, `#FFFFFF` background.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts cleanly to `#047857` accompanied by an emerald-tinted outer glow ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(4, 120, 87, 0.15)`).
- **Labels:** Set in `#0F172A` with `label-lg` sizing, paired with optional gold asterisks for required fields.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Checkboxes:** `6px` rounded corners. When checked, filled with `#064E3B` with a crisp white checkmark icon.
- **Radio Buttons:** Circular, styled with `#064E3B` inner dot and outer ring when active.

### Lists & Schedules
- **Prayer Times Schedule Bar:** Tabular horizontal grid with active prayer highlighted in `#F0FDF4`, bordered in `#047857`, featuring an illuminated gold countdown indicator.
- **Knowledge/Lecture Feed:** Divided by hairlines (`#F1F5F9`), utilizing metadata stamps in muted slate (`#475569`) with play/listen triggers highlighted in tertiary green.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Zakaat & Sadaqah Calculator:** A stepped modular interface featuring interactive monetary inputs, preset quick-giving pill triggers (`$25`, `$50`, `$100`, `$500`), and a clear amber total summary badge.
- **Ayah / Hadith Citation Callout:** Contained within a `#F0FDF4` ivory-mint card with delicate gold corner ornaments and an italic Playfair Display quote format.
